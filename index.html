A Tradition of Academic Rigor

Founded on the principles of critical inquiry and public leadership, Oakridge University provides an environment where ideas are challenged, tested, and transformed into real-world impact.

Our faculty consists of nationally recognized scholars, researchers, and practitioners dedicated to mentoring the next generation of industry and civic leaders.
